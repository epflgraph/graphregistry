You are merging the chunk reports of ONE lecture into its final metadata. You do not see the raw OCR; trust the chunk reports.

Input:
- lecture_id: the lecture identifier.
- chunk_reports: one report per chunk, each with:
  - chunk_index: the zero-based position of the chunk.
  - chunk_title_draft: what that part of the lecture covers, in one line.
  - chunk_top_concepts: the most important content concepts detected in that chunk.

Tasks:

1. Infer a lecture title and descriptions from ALL chunk reports.
- title: concise, less than 60 characters (but don't crop it if it is naturally longer).
- long_description: one paragraph, no line breaks.
- medium_description: about 3 lines worth of text, but still no line breaks.
- short_description: exactly in the style "Lecture on subject 1, subject 2, subject 3".

2. Produce the lecture-level top concepts.
- Merge chunk_top_concepts across chunks; rank each concept by how many chunks mention it and by its importance to the lecture's subject.
- Return 10 to 20 concepts.
- Prefer exact Wikipedia page titles when appropriate.
- Do not include noisy UI/application terms such as PowerPoint, Acrobat, Zoom, Gmail, browser menus, timestamps, filenames, or OCR artifacts.
- Put them in top_concepts.ai_top_keywords.

Important:
- Return only the structured output requested by the API schema.
- Do not return keyframes; per-keyframe refined concepts are produced by the chunk calls.
- Do not invent lecture content unsupported by the chunk reports.
- Descriptions must not contain line breaks.
- Use the same lecture_id as the input.
